# Drinklivery App Brief

## Product

Drinklivery is a premium cocktail delivery platform for scheduled cocktail experiences.

Initial market: Panama.

Core positioning:

`Premium cocktail experiences delivered.`

Alternative customer-facing phrase:

`The bar arrives at your home.`

## What Drinklivery Sells

MVP sells:

- Ready-to-serve cocktail packs
- Cocktail kits
- Mocktail packs
- Event-oriented bundles

MVP does not sell:

- Standalone liquor bottles
- Marketplace inventory
- Generic convenience alcohol delivery

## Target Use Cases

- Homes
- Airbnbs
- Birthdays
- After-office gatherings
- Private meetings
- Small events
- Date nights
- Premium home plans

## Operating Model

Software owner:

- Adrian / Lazy Software

Panama operator:

- Local bartender/operator
- Responsible for permits, preparation, sourcing, sanitary handling, recipes, inventory, delivery coordination, and alcohol compliance

## Product Experience

The experience must feel:

- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Controlled
- Event-oriented
- Mobile-first
- Compliance-aware

Avoid positioning that feels like a cheap liquor delivery app.
