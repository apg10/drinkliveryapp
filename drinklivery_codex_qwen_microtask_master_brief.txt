================================================================================
1. PROJECT SUMMARY
================================================================================

Project name: Drinklivery

Drinklivery is a premium cocktail delivery platform focused on ready-to-serve cocktail packs, cocktail kits, mocktail packs and event-oriented bundles.

The initial pilot market is Panama.

The product must NOT be positioned as a liquor bottle delivery app. The MVP does not sell standalone liquor bottles. The product is a premium cocktail experience delivered to homes, Airbnbs, small events, birthdays, after-office gatherings and private meetings.

Core concept:
- Digital-first premium cocktail brand.
- Local operation handled by a bartender/operator in Panama.
- Software owned by Lazy Software / Adrian.
- Operation may use the software under agreement, license, participation or pilot arrangement.
- The software must remain reusable for future markets.

Strategic principle:
Drinklivery is not only a cocktail business. It is a reusable software vertical for premium beverage/event delivery operations.

================================================================================
2. OWNERSHIP AND BUSINESS STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Software ownership:
- The app, backend, frontend, database structure, operational workflows, API design, admin panel, driver/compliance logic and future SaaS capabilities belong to Adrian / Lazy Software.
- The Panama operating partner does not automatically own the software.

Panama operating partner:
- Local bartender/operator.
- Responsible for permits, local preparation, recipes, inventory, suppliers, sanitary handling, local delivery coordination and alcohol-related compliance.
- Responsible for validating what is legally required in Panama before real sales.

Business separation:
1. Software platform: owned by Lazy Software / Adrian.
2. Local operation: handled by Panama operator.
3. Brand: should be structured carefully so it can expand beyond Panama.

Recommended positioning:
"Premium cocktail experiences delivered."

Operational language:
Use "authorized micro-lab for premium cocktail delivery and scheduled digital orders" instead of informal language like "selling cocktails from home."

================================================================================
3. MVP BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

These rules are locked for the MVP:

1. Drinklivery does not sell standalone liquor bottles in the MVP.
2. Drinklivery sells:
   - ready-to-serve cocktail packs
   - cocktail kits
   - mocktail packs
   - event-oriented bundles
3. Orders are scheduled by default, not instant-delivery-first.
4. Customers must confirm legal drinking age before placing alcoholic orders.
5. Alcoholic orders require in-person delivery/handoff.
6. Alcoholic orders cannot be left at reception, door, lobby, concierge or with minors.
7. The admin/operator can reject orders if area, schedule, availability or compliance rules are not met.
8. MVP starts with one tenant: Drinklivery Panama.
9. The system must be architected as single-market MVP, multi-market-ready.
10. Payment integrations are not part of the first backend milestones.
11. WhatsApp API integration is not part of the first backend milestones.
12. The initial system may use manual payment tracking.
13. The app must support delivery zones and scheduled delivery windows.
14. The app must support order state tracking.
15. Compliance data must be handled carefully. Do not store sensitive ID document images in the MVP.

================================================================================
4. COMPLIANCE RULES
================================================================================

Compliance rules for MVP:

1. Customer must confirm legal drinking age before placing an alcoholic order.
2. Alcoholic orders require in-person handoff.
3. Receiver document must be checked at delivery.
4. If receiver is underage, unavailable or visibly intoxicated, the order must not be delivered.
5. The system must allow marking an order as FAILED_AGE_VERIFICATION.
6. Delivery verification must record:
   - receiver name
   - whether document was checked
   - adult verification result
   - timestamp
   - operator/driver/admin who verified
7. The app must display responsible drinking messaging.
8. The app must support configurable operating hours.
9. The app must support disabling orders for restricted dates or unavailable dates later.
10. MVP must avoid storing sensitive document images.
11. Legal implementation details must be verified locally by the Panama operating partner before launch.

13. PUBLIC API ENDPOINTS
================================================================================

Initial public storefront endpoints:

GET /api/health/

GET /api/public/{tenant_slug}/catalog/
- returns active categories/products/variants for tenant
- excludes inactive products
- ordered by display_order

GET /api/public/{tenant_slug}/products/{product_slug}/
- returns product detail if active and belongs to tenant

GET /api/public/{tenant_slug}/delivery-zones/
- returns active delivery zones for tenant

POST /api/public/{tenant_slug}/orders/
- creates customer/address/order/order items
- validates cart
- validates active product/variant
- validates delivery zone
- validates age confirmation when alcoholic products are present
- validates terms acceptance
- calculates subtotal, delivery fee and total
- creates order with PENDING status

GET /api/public/{tenant_slug}/orders/{order_code}/status/
- returns safe public order status
- does not expose admin/internal details
